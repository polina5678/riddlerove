The Manor Mystery

Lord Ashcombe was found dead in his study at midnight. Four guests, one culprit. Read the clues and decide who lied.

The case

At 11:50 PM the lights in Ashcombe Manor flickered and went out for about five minutes. When the butler came with a candle, Lord Ashcombe lay dead at his desk. The study window was locked from the inside and the door was unlocked. Only the four dinner guests were in the house.

Clues

The study clock was smashed and stopped at 11:52.
A woman's wet footprint was found in the hallway near the study door. The rain had stopped at 11:30.
Lord Ashcombe's letter opener was missing. A glass of red wine stood untouched on the desk, and Lord Ashcombe never drank red wine.
The fuse box in the cellar had been switched off by hand, not by a storm.

The suspects

Lady Marlowe, the widow's sister

“I was in the conservatory with a book until the lights went out. I stayed there, then felt my way upstairs. I never went near the study.” Her shoes were dry and clean.

Dr. Fenwick, the family doctor

“I was in the library. When the lights failed I stayed in my chair. I only came out when I heard the butler shouting.” He drinks only red wine.

Miss Alder, the secretary

“I went out to the garden at 11:20 for fresh air and came back inside around 11:35 because of the rain. After that I was in the hall.” Her shoes were by the front door, damp.

Mr. Crane, the business partner

“I was in the cellar checking the wine cellar for a bottle. The darkness caught me there, and I could not find my way up for a while.”

Your verdict

Who killed Lord Ashcombe?

How to play

Read the clues carefully: one suspect's story does not match the evidence. Pick the person you think did it. You have two guesses, and a hint is shown after a wrong one. This mystery is original fiction, and every character is invented.
