About RiddleRove

RiddleRove is a small independent website for people who love a good mystery. We build free browser games, whodunit puzzles and detective quizzes that you can play in a few minutes on your phone or computer, with no download and no sign-up.

What you will find here

  • Mystery games: read the clues, question the suspects and name the culprit.
  • Detective quizzes: short, shareable tests about your instincts and observation skills.
  • Riddles and logic puzzles: quick challenges for a coffee break.

How we make our content

Every case, character and story on RiddleRove is original fiction. Names, places and events are invented, and any resemblance to real people or situations is coincidental. We write and test each mystery so that it can be solved by careful reading and logic alone.

How the site is funded

RiddleRove is free to use. The site is supported by advertising, which helps us cover hosting and keep adding new cases. You can read how advertising and cookies work in our Privacy Policy.
